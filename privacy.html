Information collected

When you submit an appointment request, the form collects your name, telephone number, email address, requested service, preferred appointment date and time, number of people, and any optional note you provide. Please do not include medical history or other sensitive health information in the optional note.

How the clinic uses your information

The clinic uses these details to respond to your request and arrange an appointment. Sending a request does not confirm an appointment; clinic staff will contact you.

Storage and service providers

Appointment requests are stored in a managed database hosted in Singapore. The website and database providers and their authorized personnel may process information to operate, maintain, and secure the service. The clinic should confirm the providers and any required safeguards before accepting real requests.

Retention and deletion

Requests do not expire automatically. The clinic retains them until the owner deletes them, unless applicable law or a valid request to withdraw consent or erase information requires earlier deletion. The owner can delete a request from the private appointment dashboard. Deletion removes it from the active booking database; backup copies, if any, may remain temporarily under the hosting provider's backup cycle.

Your choices

You may contact the clinic by telephone to ask about access to or correction of your information, withdraw consent, or request deletion. The clinic may need to retain or process some information where applicable law requires it.
